### <a name="food-com"></a> Rice (Cơm)

#### [Cơm gà bà Thuỷ](https://share.google/lpQ7m7AdChxZhCmBo)

- **Address:** 279 Chính Hữu
- **Price:** 45k – 70k
- **Must try:** Hand-torn boiled chicken rice
- Family-run place with quality chicken at a fair price. Order the boiled chicken with herbs, cucumber, tomato and kimchi. Order soup separately if eating with family - the seaweed soup with big shrimp is great.

#### [Cơm gà Hồng Ngọc](https://share.google/dB9BS4xg5DP9phOMa)

- **Address:** 193 Nguyễn Chí Thanh
- **Price:** 45k – 70k
- **Must try:** Chicken rice

## <a name="sight-seeing"></a> 🚶 Walks & Sightseeing

### [Sunrise swim at Phạm Văn Đồng beach](https://goo.gl/maps/vHKAMjMgkCVrSaxt5)

- **Address:** Biển Phạm Văn Đồng
- **Price:** Parking 4k + 2k shower / person
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer
- Ideal in summer - cool water and few people. Park at lot no. 3 (farthest, fewest people). Go 4:30–5:30AM to see sunrise next to Son Tra.

### Han River walk (Bạch Đằng)

- **Address:** Bạch Đằng
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer
- **Hours & notes:** Best in the evening.
- Cool and lively in the evening; a bit empty and sunny in the morning. Park at APEC park.

### Nguyễn Văn Trỗi pedestrian bridge

- **Address:**
  Có 2 hướng đi bộ lên cầu:
  1. Chân cầu Trần Thị Lý (buổi tối đông đúc, có bán nước và đồ ăn vặt). Cứ xếp xe vào rồi đi chơi, không thấy ai giữ xe.
  2. Đi bộ từ khu đảo xanh qua (không nên đi hướng này vì vắng vẻ, không ai trông xe).
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer
- Wide, breezy and beautiful at sunset; in the evening Gen Z hang out in small groups. Use the Trần Thị Lý side - the other approach is deserted.

### [Son Tra walk: Guard Station to Vọng Cảnh Hill](https://goo.gl/maps/tAZFKyfau9j63B1LA)

- **Address:** Bắt đầu từ ["Trạm Canh Gác Bán Đảo Sơn Trà"](https://goo.gl/maps/TxacQaqZGgYsmAzv8) đến [Đồi Vọng Cảnh](https://goo.gl/maps/tAZFKyfau9j63B1LA)
- **Must try:** Coconut at the viewpoint
- Great walking route: little traffic, sometimes langurs, monkeys and birds, new sim-flower road, views over the city. ~2–3km up to Vọng Cảnh. Go in the morning; the afternoon sun is hot. Scooters are stopped at the station - you need a manual motorbike to go further.

### City walk: Nguyễn Chí Thanh - Lê Lợi loop

- **Address:** Nguyễn Chí Thanh – Lê Lợi
- **Must try:** i3 cafe sữa
- **Hours & notes:** Best 7–9AM.
- Tree-lined loop (~1.5–2km) with sidewalks; watch daily life. Passes Cà phê Long and a bún chả cá place.

### Scenic motorbike loops

- **Address:** Nguyễn Chí Thanh (one-way, from Lê Duẩn to the end); Phan Châu Trinh (from Nguyễn Văn Linh to the end); Thanh Thủy (from Ông Ích Khiêm to 3/2)
- Tree-lined, cool streets for an easy motorbike ride.

### [Sunset at Wonder Park (Thuận Phước bridge)](https://g.page/wonder-park-danang?share)

- **Address:** Đối diện [Đà Nẵng Golden Bay Hotel](https://g.page/wonder-park-danang?share)
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer
- Go around 5:30–6PM in summer; sit on the embankment by the Thuận Phước bridge foot. Combine with a bowl at Bánh canh Cô Cúc and a night walk on the Han River.

## <a name="treaking"></a> ⛰️ Hiking

- **Address:** Sân thượng Hải Vân — Hải Vân Quan

### [Ghềnh Bàng hike (Son Tra)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HviFT5R8kU6bS7p39)

- **Address:** Ghềnh Bàng, Sơn Trà
- **Price:** 10k – 30k (giữ xe, uống nước)
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer

## <a name="hoi-an"></a> 🏮 Hội An

### [Cà phê Hoa Lúa](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GpR6HVRydNHiHfTg9)

- **Address:** Khối An Mỹ, Hội An Đông, Đà Nẵng
- **Price:** 20k – 50k
- **Best season:** ☀️ Summer, 🌸 Spring
- Airy cafe for sunset and walking through the fields; best in the young-rice and ripe-rice seasons.

### [Cháo nghêu Cô Gió](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jtLLRHHd9jrBoBLJ8)

- **Address:** Biển An Bàng, Hội An
- **Price:** 30k – 50k
- **Must try:** Clam porridge (or bone porridge)
- **Hours & notes:** Open all day, closes 6PM.
- Warming bowl before walking Hoi An. Locals think Da Nang clams are better, but it's good, fair and busy. Prefer bone porridge if you don't like farmed clams.

### [Ốc hương sả tắc](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vFqEZ5S7RCACJAXZ8)

- **Address:** 155 Tôn Đức Thắng, Hội An Tây, Đà Nẵng
- **Price:** 80k – 150k / món
- **Must try:** Sweet snails stir-fried with lemongrass and kumquat
- **Hours & notes:** Open from 4PM.
- Best snail place in Hoi An: fresh, big sweet snails and reasonable prices. Also coconut stir-fried snails, tamarind razor clams, grilled scallops. Go early to avoid waiting.

### [Sound Of Silence Coffee Shop](https://maps.app.goo.gl/cBDFqon9jb3LWUKZ9)

- **Address:** 40 Nguyễn Phan Vinh, Hội An
- **Price:** 50k – 100k
- Our pick if you choose one cafe in Hoi An: beautiful garden and head-on sea view from above. Windy in the afternoon - bring a jacket.
