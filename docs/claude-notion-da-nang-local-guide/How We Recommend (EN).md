# 🔎 How We Recommend

🇻🇳 *Tiếng Việt: xem "How We Recommend (VI)"*

> This page explains how places get into the guide, so readers can trust the picks. Edit the wording to match how *you* really choose.

## Our principles

1. **We only list places we've been to or trust.** No paid placements.
2. **We explain the decision.** Every place has a *Why we go* note: when we'd go and what to order.
3. **We say what's not great.** Trade-offs (queues, heat, price) are part of the note.
4. **We keep it fresh.** Each place has a *Last checked* date. Anything older than 6 months gets re-verified.

## What the properties mean

| Property | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Type | The main kind of place: Food, Coffee, Nightlife, Wellness, Sights & Walks, Outdoor |
| Category | The specific kind: e.g. Bún, Mỳ Quảng, Seafood, Specialty coffee, Hike |
| Area | Where in Da Nang it is |
| Price (VND) | Typical price we paid or saw |
| Price band | ₫ under ~50k · ₫₫ ~50–100k · ₫₫₫ ~100–200k · ₫₫₫₫ 200k+ |
| Best session | When it is at its best (morning, sunset, late night...) |
| Vibe | The feel of the place (Local, Quiet, Garden, Work-friendly...) |
| Good for | Who it suits (Solo, Couple, Family, Group, Work...) |
| Must try | The one thing to order or do |
| Why we go | Our honest reason for recommending it |
| Last checked | The last date someone on the team verified it |
| Data status | Draft (needs a check) or Verified |

## Optional: Local Scorecard

If you add a scorecard, define each score in one sentence so it's explainable. Examples to adapt:

- **Localness (1–5):** how commonly Da Nang residents use this place, not how famous it is online.
- **Value (1–5):** quality and portion for the price.
- **Convenience (1–5):** ease of parking, finding it, and opening hours.
